News Flash! Ellen is currently starring as Madame Morrible in the Broadway company of Wicked. Last year she was at the August Wilson Theater playing Fraulein Schneider in the award-winning Broadway revival of Cabaret at the Kit Kat Club. In 2025, she originated Ken Ludwig’s latest comedy Lady Molly of Scotland Yard, and prior to that was seen as Helen Hubbard in Ken Ludwig’s thrilling adaptation of Murder On The Orient Express at the Old Globe Theatre. Also proud to be a part of the glorious cast of this year’s SXSW award-winning film F**ktoys, and loved playing the evil Catherine Braithwaite in Red Dead Redemption II.
